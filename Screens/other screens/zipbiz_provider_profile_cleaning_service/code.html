Frequently Asked Questions

Are your cleaning chemicals safe for pets and children? expand_more

Yes, absolutely. We use eco-friendly, non-toxic, and industry-approved cleaning products that are completely safe for kids, pets, and delicate interior surfaces.

Do ZipBiz cleaners bring their own machines? expand_more

Yes, our staff comes fully equipped with heavy-duty vacuum machines, tile scrubbers, ladders, microfibers, and professional cleaning solutions. You don't need to provide anything.

How long does a 2BHK or 3BHK deep cleaning take? expand_more

A standard 2BHK takes roughly 4–5 hours, while a 3BHK takes 6–8 hours depending on property size and condition. We send a dedicated team of 2 to 4 cleaners.
